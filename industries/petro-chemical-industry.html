Petrochemical Packaging and Heavy-Duty Plastic Pallets in UAE

NexGen Plastic Industries supplies petrochemical packaging solutions for industrial sites, warehouses, production facilities and bulk handling operations. Our range includes heavy-duty plastic pallets and industrial containers designed for stable stacking, repeated use and dependable movement of materials in demanding environments.
