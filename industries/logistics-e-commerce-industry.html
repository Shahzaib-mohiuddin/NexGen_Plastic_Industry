NexGen Plastic Industries supplies reusable logistics and e-commerce packaging for warehouses, distribution centres, fulfilment operations, online retail supply chains, and industrial transport across the UAE, GCC, and Africa. Our range includes plastic pallets, stackable crates, nestable containers, foldable bulk solutions, and custom packaging designed to keep logistics and e-commerce operations fast, clean, and cost-efficient. Built for repeated use, these solutions help improve throughput, reduce handling issues, save storage space, and support long-term operational efficiency in both manual and automated environments.

Meeting the needs of modern warehousing

Supporting automation and system integration

Our packaging is designed for smooth handling in automated warehouses and distribution centres. Consistent footprints and robust designs ensure seamless compatibility with conveyors, robotics and automated storage systems.

Maximising transport and storage efficiency

Stackable, nestable and foldable options help you make the most of every cubic metre. Our products are built to optimize space utilisation in trucks, warehouses and retail environments.

Reducing costs and environmental impact

Reusable packaging lasts for years, cutting replacement costs and reducing waste. Made from recyclable materials, our solutions support your sustainability goals while improving your bottom line.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our logistics and warehousing packaging solutions.

What types of logistics packaging do you offer?

We offer innovative stackable products, foldable solutions, pallet products, and custom solutions designed for distribution centers, warehouses, and supply chain operations.

Are your products compatible with automated systems?

Yes. Our products are designed with consistent footprints and robust construction for seamless integration with conveyors, robotics, and automated storage systems.

How do foldable products save costs?

Foldable products collapse flat when empty, reducing return transport costs by up to 75% and saving valuable warehouse storage space.

How durable are your logistics products?

Our products are built for long‑term use, providing reliable performance for many years of repeated handling and transport cycles.

Are your products recyclable?

Yes. All our packaging is designed to be 100% recyclable at the end of its useful life, supporting sustainable logistics operations.
