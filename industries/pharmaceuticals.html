Pharmaceutical Packaging in UAE for Medicines, Medical Devices and Cold Chain

NexGen Plastic Industries supplies pharmaceutical packaging solutions for medicine handling, medical devices, laboratory support and temperature-sensitive distribution. Our reusable pharma crates, plastic pallets and precision containers are designed to help protect sensitive products from contamination, damage and environmental exposure during storage and transport.

Built for hygienic operations, our pharmaceutical packaging supports traceability, clean handling and compatibility with controlled environments. The range includes containers for medicines and medical supplies, trays for organized handling, and cold-chain support options for products that require stable transport conditions. Durable, easy-clean materials also support long-term reuse and operational efficiency.

We support pharmaceutical and healthcare buyers across the UAE and export orders to GCC and African markets. Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our healthcare and pharmaceutical packaging solutions.

What types of pharmaceutical packaging do you offer?

We offer innovative pharmaceutical products, medical trays, temperature-controlled packaging, and custom solutions for medications, vaccines, and medical devices.

Are your products compliant with pharmaceutical regulations?

Yes. Our packaging meets strict pharmaceutical industry standards and regulatory requirements for safety, hygiene, and traceability.

Can your packaging be used in cleanroom environments?

Yes. Our products are designed for cleanroom compatibility, with smooth surfaces that are easy to clean and resistant to contamination.

Do you offer temperature-controlled packaging?

Yes. We provide insulated products and cold chain solutions for temperature-sensitive pharmaceuticals and vaccines.

Are your products recyclable?

Yes. All our packaging is designed to be 100% recyclable at the end of its useful life, supporting sustainable healthcare operations.
